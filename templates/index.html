Help

How do I add an expense?

Open Add expense, write what you spent on, enter the amount and press Find category. Check the category and press Save expense.

What if the category is wrong?

Pick the right one from the dropdown before saving. You can also edit any expense later from Transactions.

Which categories are there?

Food, Travel, Shopping, Education, Entertainment and Bills & Utilities.

Can I add several items at once?

Add them one at a time, each with its own amount. That keeps your totals correct.

How does the budget work?

Set a monthly budget in Profile. Home shows how much you have used this month.

Is my data private?

Yes. Each account sees only its own expenses, and everything is stored on the computer running SpendWise.

About this project

Introduction

SpendWise reads a short expense description, predicts one of six categories with a trained machine-learning model, and lets you confirm or fix it before saving.

Problem and objectives

Tracking expenses by hand takes effort and the same purchase is described in many ways. SpendWise classifies expense text, gives a simple website to manage expenses, and shows spending summaries.

Technology and method

Python, Flask, SQLite, scikit-learn, Pandas, Joblib, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Chart.js. Labelled CSV → TF-IDF (fit on training data only) → Multinomial Naive Bayes → evaluation on held-out data → saved model loaded by Flask.

Limitations

The dataset is synthetic and limited, so unusual wording can be mis-classified. Only six categories and INR amounts. No bank or payment connection.
